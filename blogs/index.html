Reader help

Open a post: add ?id=blog003 (or other id) to the URL. Examples: / (index), /?id=blog003

TTS / bottom bar: Press t or the Play button to start. The bottom bar is the only TTS control; it shows progress, voice, speed and volume. While TTS plays the currently read sentence is highlighted. Use the progress slider to jump around. The TTS state (voice, speed, last segment) is saved and resumes.

Reading ruler: enable "Reading ruler" from the hub to get a soft band. The first time you enable it you'll see a short overlay showing how to use it; you can dismiss it. It follows the viewport and helps keep your eyes on the current line.

Hub (bottom-right): this is always visible. Click to expand or press m. Hub contains text size, spacing, font, high contrast, ruler, reduced motion, export and copy link.

Images: images are loaded directly as the JSON `src` provides. If an image fails to load the reader will silently fall back to your logo so layout stays intact.

Keyboard shortcuts: m hub, t play/pause, [/] previous/next post, +/- text size.
